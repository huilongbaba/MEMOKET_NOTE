## 用固定复核把速度转化为可靠性

可以采用下面的顺序：

1. Write the personal notes, ideas, and to-dos in OneNote.
2. Use those notes when developing the card or one-pager.
3. After writing the reasoning or text, read it again from the beginning.
4. Compare the finished version with the earlier card or one-pager, checking that the recorded information has been carried through and looking specifically for names, words, instructions, and other details that are easy to miss.

This keeps the positive part of moving quickly—the energy to begin and continue—while adding a fixed pause at the point where omissions are most likely to appear.

When explaining this in an interview, the weakness should be presented as a pattern that is being managed, not as a fixed character trait. A clear answer would be: “I am motivated to move quickly, but sometimes I go through information too fast and miss small details. I now write down the important points and review my final work against those notes before I move on.” This keeps the focus on a specific risk and the steps taken to reduce it, rather than labeling the whole person as careless.
